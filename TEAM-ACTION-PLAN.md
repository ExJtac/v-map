## Candidate research rules

The [candidate research plan](CANDIDATE-RESEARCH-PLAN.md) and [evidence schema](CANDIDATE-EVIDENCE-SCHEMA.json) define the detailed workflow. Apply the same standards to Republicans and Democrats and retain other-party winners when applicable. Research may begin as an internal provisional-candidate draft, but must not turn a reported lead into a confirmed-winner assertion.

Election pattern flags do not establish facts about a candidate’s conduct. Candidate background research must not be presented as proof of a county voting anomaly.

Cover relevant public news, official career and voting records, campaign-finance/disclosure records, verified public social accounts and lawfully available public archives. Include both favorable and unfavorable relevant material and its original context. An allegation, charge, finding, dismissal and correction are different events and must stay distinct.

For Wayback and other public archives, retain the original URL/post identifier, archive URL, capture time, original publication time when known, retrieved bytes/hash when permitted, and verification notes. A snapshot is not a complete account history or proof that every embedded resource belongs to the capture date. A missing post is not proof of deletion. Use the plan's corroboration and review gates before labeling something deleted or attributing it to a person.

This plan does not authorize recovering private/deleted material through credentials, leaks or access-control bypasses. It excludes private contact/home/family/minor information and speculative sensitive-trait inferences. No dossiers or allegations about named individuals were produced in this planning pass.
