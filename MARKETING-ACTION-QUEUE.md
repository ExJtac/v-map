## Demonstration checklist

- Use a route the current app can restore; include county, office and year in the caption anyway. For example, the shipped app accepts `?office=governor&year=2026&county=48479`; this is a navigation example, not a recommended claim about that county.
- Record current source values and the retrieval/update date; verify an original source immediately before a substantive caption. Do not reuse an old snapshot as a newly counted result.
- Keep axes, baselines, denominator and provisional/held labels visible. A large count change does not identify its cause, and a peer frequency is not the odds of fraud.
- Use the V-Map logo for the share image. Keep the donation QR in the support flow.
- Review the complete post, destination, channel rules, alt text and account ownership before any external send.
