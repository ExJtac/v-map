U.S. election data coverage, 2006–2026

Local data snapshot: 2026-11-06. Publication is not a live election-results feed.

Exact imported coverage for U.S. President, Senate, House and Governor elections in V-Map’s 2006–2026 window. Coverage varies by office, year, state, county, ballot round and source quality.

2026 coverage is incomplete and provisional. Imported totals are dated snapshots, not certified national results. Review borders identify changes worth checking; they do not establish fraud, an error, or an election outcome. Missing data is never treated as zero.

Geographic and source limits

  • Counts describe imported records, not an assertion every election or county is covered.
  • Alaska reports by election district; no unsupported county allocation is made.
  • County and district boundaries change over time; 2017 map geometry is a reference.
  • Unopposed races with no recorded vote totals remain distinct from missing sources.
  • 2026 records are dated provisional snapshots; no certified outcome or race call is inferred.

House comparisons currently use only verified Montana and Arizona scopes. Alaska’s election-district returns are not allocated to counties. Historical FIPS changes, Connecticut county definitions, Hawaii jurisdiction differences and changing congressional boundaries require explicit treatment; reference-map presence alone proves neither comparability nor completeness.
