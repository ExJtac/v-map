    About coverage & historical boundaries

    Years run from 2006 through 2026. Only imported source records are shown; an empty year does not prove that no election occurred. House district results are kept separate from county results. The map uses 2017 county boundaries, so county changes and historical district boundaries may not match every election year. Alaska boroughs, Louisiana parishes, and independent cities appear as county equivalents. Presidential colors represent popular votes, not Electoral College outcomes.

    County history spans presidential cycles from 2008–2024 and imported Senate, House, and Governor contests from 2006 onward, including off-cycle and recall elections. County House records identify each county's portion of a district. Source-specific gaps and review holds remain visible. D.C. has presidential records but no selectable polygon.

    2026 snapshots include published unofficial returns and source update times. Precinct reporting progress is separate from ballot completion and certification. This data snapshot updates when source data are imported; it is not a continuously updating feed. View data sources and import coverage ↗ 2018 county source notes ↗
