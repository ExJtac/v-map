CANDIDATE ACCOUNTABILITY · 2026 PILOT

What they said.
What they did.

Original statements, their context, and the documented record. Follow the full sources and draw your own conclusions.

Same evidence standards. Every party. Any topic.

This dated manual pilot covers 12 candidates; it is not a complete campaign, social-media or governing history. A statement is not an action, a bill introduction is not an enacted policy, and an election-day result is not a sworn-in date. Post-election statements remain separate from official acts. Unknown dates and unavailable records stay visible.

These records do not assign kept/broken-promise, honesty or overall-priority scores. Review limitations before interpreting a comparison.

Read the record in context

Words and authorship. Campaign website text is attributed to the campaign. It is not automatically a transcript of the candidate speaking. Short excerpts link to the full original text, interview, thread or recording when verified.

Actions and authority. Votes retain their exact question and legislative stage. Prior-office actions are dated as prior records. A prospective commitment is not broken simply because the new term has not begun.

Archives and missing evidence. Archive capture time differs from original publication time. A missing post is not proof its author deleted it. Account linkage does not establish that every post or the full history was reviewed.

Interpretation and corrections. Following through on a promise, reversing a position and changing priorities are different questions. A few selected records cannot establish a whole governing agenda. Corrections should identify the candidate, exact item and original source.
